The Person Behind Beautifully Well.

I am Jocelyn the founder of Beautifully Well. I have been a beauty skin care consultant for 18 years. An Amare` supplement vendor for 12 years. And lastly, both a Cosmetologist and Massotherapist for the last 4 years.

What I do in my spare time is to enjoy the Montana outdoors. With hiking, kayaking, fishing, and anything fun I can do with my family. With that, I have dedicated my practice in being family friendly with a holistic healing approach for overall inner balance and rejuvenation.

I am a National Medical Massage Therapy Provider specializing in Neuromuscular Therapy.
